WHO I AM
MEET THE PROFESSOR

A lifetime devoted to discovery, education and purposeful leadership.

Professor Sabinus Ifeanyi Ofoefule is a Nigerian professor of Pharmaceutical Technology and Industrial Pharmacy at the University of Nigeria, Nsukka. A registered pharmacist, researcher and academic leader, his work spans pharmaceutical formulation, drug delivery and the development of pharmaceutical materials.

Since joining the University of Nigeria in 1993, he has served in teaching, research, mentoring and leadership roles. He earned his B.Pharm (1988), M.Pharm (1991) and Ph.D. (1997) from the University of Nigeria and was appointed professor in 2003.
