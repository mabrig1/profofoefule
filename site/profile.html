ACADEMIC BIOGRAPHY

Science, education and service.

Professor Sabinus Ifeanyi Ofoefule is a Nigerian academic and registered pharmacist whose career has been devoted to Pharmaceutical Technology and Industrial Pharmacy at the University of Nigeria, Nsukka. He has researched pharmaceutical formulations, drug delivery systems, powder and tablet technology, novel drug-delivery materials and excipient development.

He joined UNN as a Lecturer II in January 1993, obtained a double promotion to Senior Lecturer in 1997, and attained the rank of Professor in 2003. Throughout his career, he has taught, supervised postgraduate research and held leadership responsibilities in university faculties, departments and research institutions.

Academic and institutional leadership

His documented appointments have included Acting Head of Department, Pharmaceutical Technology and Industrial Pharmacy (1998 and 2000–2002); Acting Dean, Faculty of Pharmaceutical Sciences, UNN (2007–2008); Dean, Faculty of Pharmaceutical Sciences, UNN (2008–2010); Dean, Faculty of Pharmacy, University of Uyo (2011–2012); and Head of Pharmaceutical Technology and Industrial Pharmacy, UNN (2015–2017).

He has also been publicly identified with the University of Nigeria's SERVICOM and research-leadership structures and with the Institute for Drug–Herbal Medicine–Excipient Research and Development. These records establish historical service; the exact current appointments require a recent CV.

Research interests and postgraduate mentorship

UNN postgraduate supervisor records list his expertise in powder and tablet technology, polymer-based drug delivery, drug targeting, nanoscience, biopharmaceutics, pharmacokinetics, drug analysis, phytomedicine, excipient functionality, hot-melt extrusion and pharmaceutical equipment development.

UNN public records report more than 150 research publications and four books. The NUC's 2021 directory credited him with seven completed doctoral supervisions and four ongoing Ph.D. supervisions at the time; these are historical, not live supervision statistics.

Professional affiliations

He is a registered and licensed pharmacist and a Fellow of the Pharmaceutical Society of Nigeria. UNN also lists professional affiliations including the Institute of Industrial Administration of Nigeria, the University of Nigeria Business School and the Institute of Public Analysts of Nigeria. Current honorific designations should be confirmed directly with him before final publication.
