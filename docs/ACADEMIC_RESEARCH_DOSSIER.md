## Early life and education

In an interview originally dated 22 July 2019, Professor Ofoefule described attending multiple primary schools as his father, a teacher, changed postings. He named Central School Ezeachi and Community Primary School Etam Okpuitumo (now Ebonyi State), and St Augustine's Grammar School in Imo State. He said he entered UNN in 1983 to study Pharmacy and graduated in 1988. His place/date of birth and family details have not been verified and should not be assumed.

## Editorial website biography — draft, pending his review

**Professor Sabinus Ifeanyi Ofoefule** is a Nigerian professor of Pharmaceutical Technology and Industrial Pharmacy at the University of Nigeria, Nsukka. A registered pharmacist and distinguished academic, he earned his B.Pharm in 1988, M.Pharm in 1991 and Ph.D. in 1997 from the University of Nigeria. Since joining the university's academic staff in 1993, he has developed an extensive career in pharmaceutical formulation, powder and tablet technology, polymer-based drug delivery, pharmaceutical excipients and nanotechnology. Promoted to professor in 2003, he has served in university leadership posts including departmental head and dean of pharmaceutical sciences at the University of Nigeria and dean of pharmacy at the University of Uyo. His university's public profile reports more than 150 research publications and four books. His broader contributions include postgraduate supervision, pharmaceutical research leadership, professional service, ethics and university capacity building. His research reflects a commitment to improving medicine formulation and bringing pharmaceutical innovation into practical use.
